RECOVER

Please scroll horizontally to view the number of variables and events of administration for the displayed tables.

Domain overview

The RECOVER substudy was introduced in the ABCD 7.0 data release to capture information related to COVID-19 infection, recovery, and longer-term health outcomes. Data were collected at a dedicated RECOVER event (ses-REC) with an independent event structure.

Caregivers completed separate questionnaires about the youth and themselves assessing COVID-19 infection history, symptoms, physical and mental health, health care utilization, and social determinants of health. In addition, antibody testing was conducted using at-home collection kits.

ImportantRECOVER Data and Documentation Note

Data included in this section originate from the RECOVER study (Researching COVID to Enhance Recovery), a national initiative focused on understanding, diagnosing, preventing, and treating Long COVID.

Thorough documentation for the RECOVER study is available here and should be considered the primary reference for study design, measures, and interpretation. The documentation provided here is specific to ABCD implementation and is intended to support integration with ABCD data structures and workflows.

During data collection, RECOVER variable names were used in REDCap so name_redcap field names, provided in the data dictionary align with the RECOVER data dictionary. In the released ABCD data, variables have been renamed according to ABCD naming conventions (see Variable Naming Conventions.

Youth tables

Antibody Test Results [Youth]

recover_y_atr
tabulated

The results of SARS-CoV-2 antibody testing. A small sample of capillary blood was drawn at home using the Tasso device on the upper arm (or thigh, if necessary) and sent to the RECOVER central lab for testing.

Notes and special considerations: There may be discrepancies between test results and survey answers due to staggered timing of administration. Participants received the survey link immediately upon consent, whereas the Tasso order was placed at that time and arrived at a later date. Additionally, the participant may have delayed taking the Tasso test for a period of time. Some Tasso devices malfunctioned, leading to the administration of second, and on occasion third, tests. Initial Tasso devices received hemolyzed by the lab were indicated for retesting. However, later on in the study, the lab determined hemolyzed tests were suitable for testing and resulting. As such, some participants may have more than one result.

Parent tables

Antibody Test Results [Parent]

recover_p_atr
tabulated

Provides relevant information regarding antibody test process and results using the Tasso m20 device.

Notes and special considerations: The data differentiates between antibody types. “S-type antibody” refers to antibodies typically generated in response to the COVID vaccine, whereas “N-type antibody” typically refers to antibodies generated in response to COVID infection. However, there are instances in which the self-report data and antibody data are not in alignment.

Pediatric Survey

Child Birth History [Parent]

recover_p_cbh
tabulated

Measures describing the child’s birth history. Includes prenatal, birth and postnatal health data.

Child Current Health Status [Parent]

recover_p_cchs
tabulated

Provides information about the youth’s current health status, including anthropometrics (height, weight) of both the youth and their biological parents. Questions include certain disabilities and difficulties with activities of daily living.

Notes and special considerations: Height and weight were provided as a free-text box. As such, some information may not be accurate or implausible due to data entry error by the informant.

Youth COVID Family Infection [Parent]

recover_p_cfi
tabulated

Details information on household and family’s history of COVID infections and severity.

Youth COVID Testing History [Parent]

recover_p_cth
tabulated

Perceptions of COVID testing accessibility.

Youth Demographics [Parent]

recover_p_demo
tabulated

Provides information on informant’s relationship to participant, as well as youth demographic questions. Questions include birth location, sex assigned at birth, ethnicity, and primary language.

Youth First COVID Infection History [Parent]

recover_p_fcih
tabulated

Provides information regarding youth’s first COVID infection, including date, test confirmation and presence of symptoms.

Notes and special considerations: Year of COVID infection was provided as a free-text box. As such, some dates may not be possible due to data entry error by the informant.

Youth Special Health Care Needs Screener [Parent]

recover_p_hcn
tabulated

Measures medication use by the youth as well as medical and mental health conditions. Additional information includes therapeutic services (mental, physical, occupational, speech).

Notes and special considerations: Informants were provided free-text box to list any conditions that were not listed above. Data was not reviewed for accuracy.

Youth COVID Health Consequences [Parent]

recover_p_hcq
tabulated

Measures items related to nutrition, physical activity and sleep before and during the pandemic. Includes questions on school type and academic performance.

Youth Multisystem Inflammatory Syndrome in Children (MIS-C) and Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome (POTS) [Parent]

recover_p_miscpots
tabulated

Provides information as to whether the youth has ever been diagnosed with Long COVID, POTS, or MIS-C.

Youth Most Recent COVID Infection History [Parent]

recover_p_mrci
tabulated

Branched survey that provides details of most recent COVID infection if number of infections is greater than 1. Includes details regarding diagnosis and treatment methods.

Youth Patient-Reported Outcomes Measurement Information System (PROMIS) [Parent]

recover_p_promis
tabulated

General questions related to the youth’s overall physical and mental health.

Youth COVID Symptoms [Parent]

recover_p_ps
tabulated

Comprehensive questionnaire of physical and behavioral symptoms experienced by youth before, during and after COVID infection. The questionnaire is split into sections for different body systems.

Child Social Determinents of Health [Parent]

recover_p_sdoh
tabulated

Measures social determinants of health for the youth.

Youth COVID Vaccine History [Parent]

recover_p_vacc
tabulated

Describes youth’s COVID vaccination history. If informant indicates youth received 1 or more COVID vaccinations, branching questions detail vaccine type, date, and symptoms of each vaccine dose.
